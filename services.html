How we work

A process built for ambiguity.

Most interesting AI problems aren't fully defined when you start. Our process is built to make progress anyway.

①

Frame

Sharpen the problem, identify the right metric, and agree on what "done" looks like.

②

Prototype

Build the smallest end-to-end system that proves (or disproves) the hypothesis.

③

Evaluate

Measure rigorously. Real benchmarks, real users, and the failure modes nobody wants to talk about.

④

Ship

Productionize, instrument, and hand it over with the evaluations that keep it honest in the wild.
